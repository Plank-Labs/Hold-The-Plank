Executive Summary
$PLANK is a crypto-powered training and payments platform designed to solve one of the biggest challenges in the crypto ecosystem: sustainable user onboarding with real-world usage.
Today, crypto adoption remains limited to approximately 6–7% of the global population. User acquisition is expensive, speculative, and often disconnected from everyday life. At the same time, the global fitness industry faces a different problem: gyms and training platforms struggle to build digital engagement, retain users, and monetize beyond monthly fees.
$PLANK connects these two worlds through a B2B2C model that turns gyms into physical onboarding hubs for crypto adoption.
Through a gamified training app focused on isometric workouts, users earn progression and crypto rewards by completing real physical effort. That crypto can then be used immediately to pay for something tangible: their gym membership. For most users, this becomes their first meaningful crypto transaction.
Gyms benefit by onboarding new users, receiving payments directly into their wallets, and earning additional incentives based on user activity and content creation. The platform captures value through transaction fees, a multigym membership model, premium training content, and digital assets.
Unlike traditional fitness apps or multigym platforms, $PLANK does not compete on price or convenience alone. Its core differentiation lies in combining physical discipline, community, and crypto payments into a single engagement loop that reduces acquisition costs and increases retention.
The initial go-to-market strategy focuses on gym partners operating in Argentina and Spain, providing early validation in both emerging and developed markets. The hackathon MVP includes live wallets and peer-to-peer crypto payments, demonstrating real-world utility from day one.
Over a three-year horizon, $PLANK aims to establish itself as a new onboarding rail for crypto, starting with fitness and expanding into payments, data, and additional real-world verticals.
________________


2. THE PROBLEM
2.1 Crypto onboarding lacks lifestyle relevance and everyday utility
Crypto adoption is no longer constrained by technology, but by cultural relevance and daily usefulness.
Although approximately 6–7% of the global population holds cryptocurrencies, usage remains largely episodic. For most users, crypto exists in a parallel financial universe—separate from their everyday routines, identity, and social life.
Current onboarding channels emphasize:
                                                         * trading interfaces,

                                                         * financial incentives,

                                                         * and short-term rewards.

This framing positions crypto as something to watch or speculate on, rather than something to use, live with, or identify with. As a result:
                                                            * users engage sporadically, not habitually,

                                                            * crypto remains abstract and intimidating for mainstream users,

                                                            * and long-term retention depends more on market cycles than on real behavior.

Crypto lacks natural lifestyle entry points where usage feels intuitive, social, and culturally embedded.
________________


2.2 Fitness communities represent a powerful lifestyle layer — but remain financially disconnected
In contrast, the global fitness ecosystem has become one of the strongest lifestyle-driven communities in modern consumer culture.
People who train regularly:
                                                               * build identity around discipline and consistency,

                                                               * participate in physical communities (gyms, studios, classes),

                                                               * value progress, status, and belonging,

                                                               * and integrate training into their daily routines.

While only ~3% of the global population pays for a formal gym membership, penetration reaches 15–25% in developed markets, representing tens of millions of highly engaged users with recurring spending habits.
Despite this engagement, gyms remain financially and digitally constrained:
                                                                  * payments are largely passive and transactional,

                                                                  * monetization rarely extends beyond monthly fees,

                                                                  * and gyms lack tools to create digital-native communities or economic layers around their users.

This leaves a gap between high lifestyle engagement and low financial innovation.
________________


2.3 Payments today are treated as friction, not as engagement
Across both crypto and fitness ecosystems, payments are treated as a background utility:
                                                                     * In crypto, payments are abstract and rarely tied to real-world services.

                                                                     * In fitness, payments are repetitive costs with little emotional or social meaning.

Existing platforms fail to connect:
                                                                        * community and identity,

                                                                        * daily behavior,

                                                                        * and economic participation.

As a result:
                                                                           * crypto platforms struggle to feel culturally “cool” or lifestyle-native,

                                                                           * fitness platforms struggle to evolve beyond utility,

                                                                           * and neither creates a strong loop between action, belonging, and value exchange.

________________

5. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
5.1 Product vision
$PLANK is a crypto-native lifestyle platform that connects physical training, community participation, and real-world payments into a single engagement loop.
The product is designed to:
                                                                                                                                             * onboard users into crypto through an existing lifestyle habit,

                                                                                                                                             * create real economic utility from physical activity,

                                                                                                                                             * and enable recurring payments tied to community and identity.

$PLANK is not positioned as a fitness app with crypto features, nor as a wallet with workouts. It is designed as a community-driven payments layer, where training, reputation, and spending reinforce each other.
________________


5.2 MVP — User experience
The MVP focuses on delivering a complete, real-world loop for the user, from training to payment.
Core user actions
Users can:
                                                                                                                                                * create an account,

                                                                                                                                                * perform plank-based training sessions,

                                                                                                                                                * earn reputation points called Aura,

                                                                                                                                                * progress through levels and maintain streaks,

                                                                                                                                                * participate in leaderboards and community rankings,

                                                                                                                                                * pay their gym membership or a multigym membership,

                                                                                                                                                * exchange earned value to unlock broader platform functionality.

Wallet & payments
                                                                                                                                                   * Wallet type: Hybrid, on-chain wallet integrated through a third-party provider.

                                                                                                                                                   * Payments: Peer-to-peer (P2P) wallet-to-wallet payments.

                                                                                                                                                   * Payments are executed directly between users and gyms, creating immediate real-world utility.

This allows users to perform their first meaningful crypto transaction by paying for a real service, rather than interacting with abstract financial instruments.
________________


5.3 MVP — Gym experience (B2B side)
The MVP also delivers immediate value to gyms as distribution and economic partners.
What gyms receive
                                                                                                                                                      * A dedicated on-chain wallet.

                                                                                                                                                      * Direct P2P crypto payments from users.

                                                                                                                                                      * Incentives in the form of Aura points, tied to:

                                                                                                                                                         * number of active attendees,

                                                                                                                                                         * training frequency,

                                                                                                                                                         * sustained participation over time.

Gyms can build enhanced streaks and community momentum based on member activity, reinforcing engagement without needing to manage crypto exposure or complexity.
What is intentionally excluded from the MVP
                                                                                                                                                            * No dashboard or CRM.

                                                                                                                                                            * No membership management tools.

                                                                                                                                                            * No POS hardware.

These elements are part of the roadmap and intentionally excluded to keep the MVP focused on validating payments, engagement, and community loops.
________________


5.4 Training, progression, and community mechanics
Training validation
Training sessions are validated within trusted environments, ensuring that physical activity is meaningfully tied to platform rewards.
Progression logic
                                                                                                                                                               * Users earn Aura through verified training.

                                                                                                                                                               * Aura functions as a reputation and progression currency.

                                                                                                                                                               * Aura can be exchanged for specific NFTs that unlock access to the $PLANK token.

                                                                                                                                                               * Once the NFT is acquired, users can convert Aura into $PLANK.

$PLANK can be used to:
                                                                                                                                                                  * pay gym memberships,

                                                                                                                                                                  * pay multigym memberships,

                                                                                                                                                                  * purchase NFTs and digital items.

Certain NFTs can also be purchased directly, without Aura, enabling additional monetization paths.
Community features
Community is embedded through:
                                                                                                                                                                     * clans (guilds),

                                                                                                                                                                     * shared progression metrics,

                                                                                                                                                                     * leaderboards,

                                                                                                                                                                     * collective streaks.

This structure makes progression and payments socially visible, reinforcing consistency and identity.

6. BUSINESS MODEL
6.1 Business model overview
$PLANK operates a B2B2C platform where gyms act as distribution partners and users engage through training, community, and payments.
The business model is designed around:
                                                                                                                                                                                                * recurring payment flows (gym memberships),

                                                                                                                                                                                                * engagement-driven monetization (training, NFTs),

                                                                                                                                                                                                * and progressive expansion of payment infrastructure.

Value is captured at multiple layers without relying on a single revenue stream, reducing dependency on market cycles and speculative behavior.
________________


6.2 Core revenue streams
6.2.1 Transaction fees on gym payments (Primary)
Gym memberships represent a predictable, recurring payment behavior.
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Users pay their gym membership or multigym membership using $PLANK.

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Payments are executed wallet-to-wallet.

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * $PLANK charges a transaction fee on each payment.

Indicative fee range:
                                                                                                                                                                                                      * 5% per transaction, depending on volume and partner agreements.

Why this matters:
                                                                                                                                                                                                         * High-frequency payments (monthly).

                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Clear unit economics.

                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Direct linkage between physical activity and financial utility.

This is the foundational revenue layer of the platform.
________________


6.2.2 Multigym membership subscriptions
$PLANK offers a multigym membership allowing users to access multiple partner gyms through a single subscription.
                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Users pay a monthly fee to $PLANK.

                                                                                                                                                                                                            * $PLANK settles usage-based payouts to gyms.

                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Pricing can be tiered based on access, location, or features.

This model:
                                                                                                                                                                                                               * increases ARPU,

                                                                                                                                                                                                               * reduces churn through flexibility,

                                                                                                                                                                                                               * and strengthens platform lock-in.

________________

This revenue stream scales with engagement rather than pure acquisition.

8. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
8.1 How to think about competition correctly
$PLANK does not compete within a single traditional category.
It operates at the intersection of:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * fitness platforms,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * multigym aggregators,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * crypto onboarding solutions,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * and payment infrastructure.

Most competitors optimize one dimension of this stack.
$PLANK’s differentiation comes from integrating all of them into a single lifestyle-driven loop.
________________


8.2 Category 1 — Multigym and fitness aggregators
Examples: Gympass / Wellhub, Fitpass, Hussle, similar platforms.
What they do well
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Aggregate supply across gyms and studios.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Offer flexibility and convenience.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Reduce friction for users who train in multiple locations.

Structural limitations
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Primarily pricing and access driven.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Limited community and identity formation.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Payments are abstracted and invisible.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * No economic participation beyond subscriptions.

These platforms optimize where users can train, but not how users engage or why they stay.
________________


8.3 Category 2 — Fitness apps and training platforms
Examples: Freeletics, Nike Training Club, Strava (partial), similar apps.
What they do well
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Create habit-forming training experiences.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Use progression, streaks, and challenges.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Build large digital user bases.

Structural limitations
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Limited integration with physical gyms.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Monetization depends on subscriptions or advertising.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Payments are disconnected from real-world services.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * No direct financial utility from activity.

These platforms create engagement, but do not close the loop between effort and economic value.
________________


8.4 Category 3 — Crypto onboarding and wallets
Examples: Centralized exchanges, wallets, payment apps.
What they do well
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Enable crypto custody and transactions.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Scale through paid acquisition and incentives.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Provide financial infrastructure.

Structural limitations
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * High CAC and weak retention.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Onboarding is abstract and financial-first.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Limited lifestyle or community integration.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Users often churn once incentives fade.

These platforms onboard wallets, but not habits.
________________


8.5 Category 4 — Move-to-earn and crypto fitness experiments
Examples: Step-based or activity-to-token models.
What they do well
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Demonstrate that physical activity can be tokenized.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Attract early crypto-native users.

Structural limitations
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Incentive-driven rather than lifestyle-driven.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Inflationary reward structures.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Weak real-world payment integration.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Low long-term sustainability.

These models prove demand for rewards, but fail to anchor value in recurring real-world payments.

8.7 Why $PLANK is hard to replicate

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Progression, streaks, and community increase switching costs.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Identity-based engagement is harder to displace than price.

Payments + data moat
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Recurring payments create predictable flows.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Verified activity data compounds in value over time.

Progressive infrastructure
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Starting simple (P2P),

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * expanding to card and POS,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * without breaking the user experience.

11. RISKS & MITIGATION
11.1 Adoption risk (users)
Risk
 Users may engage with training and gamification but not convert to crypto payments.
Mitigation
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Payments are tied to an existing expense (gym membership), not an abstract product.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * First transaction is positioned as utility, not finance.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Progression mechanics and Aura → $PLANK conversion incentivize usage naturally.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Early validation focuses specifically on training → payment conversion.

________________


11.2 Gym adoption risk (B2B side)
Risk
 Gyms may be reluctant to accept crypto payments due to perceived complexity or volatility.
Mitigation
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Hybrid wallet and third-party custody reduce operational friction.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Gyms receive payments directly without managing market exposure.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Incentives are aligned around retention and engagement, not speculation.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * POS and card settlement are planned only after P2P validation.

________________


11.3 Regulatory and compliance risk
Risk
 Crypto regulation varies significantly by market and may impact payments or token usage.
Mitigation
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Initial focus on jurisdictions with clearer crypto frameworks.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * MVP uses P2P crypto payments without offering financial advice or custody.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Progressive rollout of payment rails allows adaptation to regulatory changes.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Fiat off-ramps and POS integrations are modular and jurisdiction-specific.

________________


11.4 Token and incentive sustainability risk
Risk
 Incentive-based models may lead to inflationary dynamics or disengagement over time.
Mitigation
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Aura functions as a reputation layer, not a freely tradeable token.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Access to $PLANK is gated through NFTs and progression.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Economic value is anchored in real-world payments.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Rewards are tied to behavior and community participation, not passive activity.

________________


11.5 Execution and scalability risk
Risk
 Expanding across gyms, markets, and payment rails increases operational complexity.
Mitigation
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Focused MVP with explicit non-goals.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Playbook-based gym onboarding.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Progressive infrastructure rollout (P2P → card → POS).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Data-driven iteration before scaling headcount or spend.

________________


11.6 Market perception risk
Risk
 $PLANK may be perceived as either “too crypto” for fitness users or “too fitness” for crypto users.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Lifestyle-first branding.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Crypto positioned as infrastructure, not narrative.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Community and progression lead the user experience.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Payments integrated quietly into the flow.

The tokenomics of $PLANK are not designed to maximize speculative activity, but to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * reward consistent behavior,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * reinforce long-term engagement,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * and align incentives between users, gyms, and the protocol.

The system is built around a dual-token architecture:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Aura, a non-transferable reputation and governance layer.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * $PLANK, a transferable utility token used for payments, incentives, and economic participation.

This design directly supports the business model described in the $PLANK Business Plan, where gyms act as B2B2C distribution partners and payments represent the core activation moment.
________________

3. Aura — Reputation, Governance & Passive Income
3.1 Aura as a reputation layer
Aura is a non-transferable score earned exclusively through verified training activity.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Aura cannot be bought, sold, or transferred.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Aura can only be earned by training.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Aura reflects consistency, commitment, and community contribution.

This design anchors governance and rewards to real behavior, fully aligned with the lifestyle-driven engagement loop of the platform.
________________


3.2 Aura-based governance
Aura holders participate in protocol governance. Governance scope includes:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Economic parameters (fees and emission variables, excluding hard-coded limits).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Gamification rules and progression mechanics.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Feature prioritization and roadmap decisions.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Treasury usage and ecosystem incentives.

Governance is progressively decentralized and introduced in phases, ensuring stability during early growth.
________________


3.3 Aura and passive income distribution
5% of the network’s passive revenue is distributed semi-annually to Aura holders.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Distribution is proportional to Aura balance.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Payments are made in stablecoins.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Revenue sources include trading fees and marketplace activity.

This mechanism aligns long-term contributors with the financial success of the network without introducing sell pressure on $PLANK.
________________


4. $PLANK — Utility, Payments & Incentives
4.1 Core utility
$PLANK is the economic engine of the ecosystem and is used to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Pay gym memberships.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Pay multigym memberships.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Purchase NFTs and digital items.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Access premium training and features.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Exchange into other tokens via supported liquidity pools.

This utility directly supports the platform’s core business model: real-world payments tied to lifestyle behavior.
________________


4.2 Incentive alignment
$PLANK is distributed as an incentive for:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * verified training,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * community participation,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * and early ecosystem adoption.

Incentives are designed to bootstrap usage, not to function as permanent yield.

6.2 Emission curve
Incentives follow a halving-like emission model, reducing issuance every 6 months.
This ensures:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * strong early adoption incentives,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * decreasing inflation as the user base grows,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * alignment with the goal of reaching 750,000 paying users by Year 3.

This structure mirrors successful protocol bootstrapping strategies while remaining tied to real usage.

Trading fees apply to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * $PLANK → other token swaps.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * NFT marketplace transactions.

Fees do not apply to direct gym payments.
________________

8. Gym Economics & B2B2C Alignment
Gyms are active economic participants in the protocol.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * When a user trains via a gym’s referral QR:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * The gym receives 20% of the Aura earned by that user.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * This Aura grants:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * governance rights,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * access to relics,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * and passive income participation.

By accepting payments in $PLANK, gyms transition from service providers to on-chain stakeholders, fully aligned with platform growth.
This reinforces the B2B2C distribution model described in the Business Plan.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * decentralized exchanges (DEXs),

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * liquidity pools supported by the ecosystem treasury.

11. Sustainability & Long-Term Alignment
The tokenomics are designed to remain sustainable beyond the incentive phase by:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * decreasing emissions over time,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * anchoring demand in real-world payments,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * aligning governance with non-transferable reputation,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * and redistributing revenue rather than inflating supply.

As incentives taper, value creation shifts from rewards to utility and network participation.
________________


12. Risks & Mitigations (Tokenomics)
Risk: Incentive farming
Mitigation: Aura gating, relic sinks, training validation.
Risk: Governance capture
Mitigation: Non-transferable Aura, gradual decentralization.
Risk: Inflation pressure
Mitigation: Fixed supply, halving-like emissions, fee sinks.
Risk: Misalignment with gyms
Mitigation: Aura sharing, governance participation, revenue alignment.

$PLANK’s tokenomics are designed to support a real-world, lifestyle-driven crypto economy, where value is created through behavior, not speculation.
By aligning users, gyms, and the protocol through a dual-token system, $PLANK establishes a sustainable foundation for long-term adoption and growth.
